SCENE: Harbour wall, dusk
AMBIENCE: waves on stone, a far foghorn
Rain needles the grey water. The lighthouse beam sweeps over the boats.
ON SCREEN: Mara (soaked raincoat, worried)
MARA (worried): He said he'd be back before the lamp was lit.
SFX: boots running on wet stone
A man in a grey coat comes out of the rain, bald and broad, a pipe in his fist.
ON SCREEN: Mara (startled), the bald man (out of breath)
THE BALD MAN (grinning): Did you miss me?
INSERT: a torn logbook page, ink smeared
THE BALD MAN: I found this on the rocks.
CG: Mara and the bald man at the pier's end, the beam behind them
MARA (whispering): Then it is true.
END CG
The beam sweeps on. Neither of them moves.
---
SCENE: Lighthouse kitchen, night
AMBIENCE: none
A low room with a black iron stove and a single oil lamp. Wet coats drip by the door.
ON SCREEN: Mara (drying her hair), Kessler (sitting by the stove)
KESSLER (tired): Your father wrote it in a hurry.
MARA: Then read it to me.
