# Cover brief: The Clockless God

A narrow Parisian clock shop at dusk, its window crowded with two hundred clocks all frozen at nine, one face glowing faint against the bluish dark. A tall untidy man in a leather jacket and a woman in a mustard scarf stand outside looking in, backs to the viewer. Overhead, a dark eclipse ring hangs in a slate sky above grey rooftops. Mood: delight, a shiver, and a quiet itch to know more. Colours: brass, deep blue, warm lamp gold.
