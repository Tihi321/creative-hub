# HUB-47 source upgrade for the three idea-fable pilots

Date: 2026-11-26. Scope: only this file was written; no book was changed.

Method note: the WebFetch tool was not available in this session. Every page was opened with `curl` (browser User-Agent), plus the public metadata APIs of Crossref, NCBI E-utilities (PubMed records and abstracts), Europe PMC and the Wayback Machine. "Opened" below means the text named in the "supports?" column was actually read in this session. Several publisher pages (APA PsycNet, Elsevier, science.org, Sage, JSTOR, Zenodo, GPS World, UVA Engineering) now answer a bot with 403 or a Cloudflare/Incapsula challenge, so they count as NOT opened even though they are likely to open in a normal browser.

Legend: **opened** = read here. **not opened** = blocked or paywalled. "none" = keep the sentence as it stands.

## Summary of what the plan needs

- Every cited fact now has a non-Wikipedia source; Wikipedia is gone from all three chapters.
- Sentences that must change before anything is public (exact text in the tables):
  1. Plain Title Page [1]: "two commanding officers ... soldiers" is wrong against Thorndike's paper; plus quiz 2 stem and answer 2.
  2. Plain Title Page [2]: drop "across the board".
  3. Clock on the Hill [1]: drop "anyone comparing the two will agree".
  4. Clock on the Hill [2]: "exactly as the idea predicted" becomes "in good agreement with what relativity predicted".
  5. Second Window [1], [2], [3]: soften "almost always / hardly anyone / far more often" and the "isn't because" claim; quiz 1 "far more often".
- Still not read in full: Dion et al. 1972, Efran 1974 (both known only through open secondary papers that cite them), and the Hafele-Keating full text (abstracts only). The Nature paper is abstract only.
- Out of scope but noticed: the chapter's NIST URL works only through a 301 redirect; Kahneman, Rosen (ISBN 9780143132899) and Rovelli/Thorne Go deeper entries were not checked.
